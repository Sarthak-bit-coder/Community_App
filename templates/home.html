Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Signing up, reporting and getting alerts are all free for neighbors.

How do WhatsApp and SMS alerts work?

Add your mobile number when you sign up (or later in your account) and tick SMS, WhatsApp or both. You'll only get alerts for your own ZIP code, and you can switch them off at any time.

How does Lost & Found matching work?

When someone posts a found item, we alert everyone with an open lost post in the same ZIP and category. If you see your item, tap "This is mine" and the finder gets your contact details.

Who can see my email and phone?

Contact details on a safety report are shown with the report. For Lost & Found, your details are only shared with the person you choose to respond to.

Who posts the important updates?

Verified Neighborhood Watch admins. Urgent updates also show as a banner across the site.
