Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Signing up, reporting and getting alerts are free.

How do text and WhatsApp alerts work?

Add your mobile number in your account and choose text, WhatsApp or both. You only get alerts for your own ZIP code, and you can turn them off any time.

Who can see my email and phone number?

For Lost & Found, your details are only shared with the person you choose to respond to.

Where does the news come from?

Local news sites are checked automatically. Only stories about incidents near you make it onto the map, and they're removed once the issue is resolved.
